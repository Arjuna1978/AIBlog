## Before Starting

Creating a Skfl Account and Logging In While in alpha, onboarding to Skfl is manual. As a first step, please navigate to [https://dev.skfl.tech] and follow the prompts to either create a new account or login using your Google or Microsoft account. This will initially return an "Access Denied" page - email [alpha@skfl.tech] the email address that you used to log in and we'll complete the provisioning process.

## Example Projects

Your account will be pre-provisioned with 6 example projects - 3 that have had source generated against them, and 3 that have not.
